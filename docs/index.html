Learning biostatistics in public.

I am working through biostatistics, quantitative genetics, and machine-learning-driven genomics from the ground up, and writing down what I understand as I understand it. The essays live on Substack. The interactive pieces live here, where I can build the thing I am trying to learn and let you poke at it too.

Read the essays on Substack Open the interactive visualization View the source on GitHub

Why learn in public? Writing for an audience forces me to turn a vague feeling of "I think I get it" into a claim I can defend. If an explanation is hand-wavy here, it is hand-wavy in my head. The visualizations are the same idea in another medium: if I can make the math move, I probably understand the math.
